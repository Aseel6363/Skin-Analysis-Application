# Glow Up AI — Intelligent Skincare Analysis & Personalized Recommendations
Glow Up AI is an intelligent mobile application that uses Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision to analyze facial skin and provide personalized skincare recommendations.
The system uses Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to detect:

* Skin type (oily, dry, normal, combination, sensitive)

* Skin tone and undertone

* Visible skin concerns such as acne, pigmentation, wrinkles, and enlarged pores

Based on the analysis, the app generates a custom daily skincare routine and recommends suitable products tailored to each user’s skin condition. For female users, the system also provides cosmetic product matching, including foundation and concealer shade suggestions.

Glow Up AI supports Arabic and English, offers a simple and user‑friendly interface, and includes a progress tracking feature that compares before‑and‑after results over time.

# Key Features
* AI Skin Analysis

* Personalized Skincare Routine

* Progress Tracking

# Project Goal
* Glow Up AI aims to make skincare smarter, more accurate, and more accessible by helping users:

* Understand their skin better

* Choose the right products

* Avoid harmful or unsuitable skincare choices

* Track their skin’s improvement over time

# Summary
Glow Up AI bridges the gap between AI technology and personal skincare by offering accurate skin analysis, personalized routines, cosmetic matching, and progress tracking — all within a simple, multilingual mobile application.
